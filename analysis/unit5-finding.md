# Unit 5 Finding: Titles With Spam Problems and Titles Nobody Has Rated

To: the leadership team
From: Sinan Rahman, Trust and Data team

In Unit 4, two titles stood out for spam: Title_02, where 3 of its 16 ratings were spam, and Title_03, where 2 of its 13 ratings were spam. That analysis only looked at titles that had ratings, so it missed two titles that have never been rated at all, Title_14 and Title_15. These two groups have different problems and need different responses.

Title_02 and Title_03 are active, and users are rating them. Both are Comedy titles released in 2023, but other Comedy titles and other 2023 releases have little or no spam. Title_05, for example, is a 2023 Comedy release with more ratings than either of them and no spam. The data does not point to anything about the titles themselves, so the spam more likely comes from the accounts that rated them, though that still needs to be checked. I recommend having the trust and safety team review the users behind the spam ratings on these two titles, and remove or restrict those accounts if the spam is confirmed. The titles themselves do not need to change.

Title_14 and Title_15 have no spam problem because they have no ratings at all. Both have been listed as available since early 2022, and neither has a genre assigned, so users who browse by genre would never see them. That is a likely reason, but not a confirmed one. Title_13 also has no genre and still has 11 ratings, so the titles could also be hard to find in search or just not interesting to users. I recommend having the catalogue team check that these two titles show up in search and browsing, and assign them genres. If they still get no ratings after that, we should decide whether they are worth keeping in the catalogue.
